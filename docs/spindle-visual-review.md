# Spindle visual development review

2026-09-06, actual Chromium application with a test-only illustrative trajectory.
Three.js 0.180 / React Three Fiber 9.3 stack; demand rendering; no shader animation
or autonomous camera easing. These are visual development observations, not a
scientific or full-plan acceptance certificate.

Draft oblique, aster detail and 390×844 mobile PNGs were opened as pixels. The
cell was too small on desktop and the center became a white knot of intersecting
filaments. The close view showed pole cores clearly but lacked aster hierarchy.

Revision enlarged the desktop composition, reduced line intensity and strongly
dimmed unselected asters. The illustrative fixture curves were made gentler and
more coherent. The matching oblique/detail/mobile PNGs were opened again: all four
cores now separate from the line field and the selected amber core has a readable
fan. Mobile enlargement clipped the far cortex; the final camera fit adds margin
at narrow widths. Scientific coordinates are unchanged by camera/light revisions.

Final, revision and draft contact sheets (front/detail/oblique, start/middle/end,
multipolar, fine-line, presentation and mobile) were opened as pixels, followed
by the full-resolution final mobile image. The narrow viewport now preserves
the cortex silhouette. Time-series stills retain coherent pole progression;
a dense movie-frame review remains outstanding. This accepts the initial art
spike only, with no claim of full visual acceptance. Simultaneous comparison, persistent pole labels, motor
fields, context-loss recovery and measured performance remain delivery work.
