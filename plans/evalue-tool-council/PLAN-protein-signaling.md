## Biological operations and first endpoint

Build a reusable measured-protein workspace with `profile(cohort, panel, assay, encoder)`, `neighbors(profile, reference_cohort, k)`, `compare(cohort, context_A, context_B, panel)` and `sites(cohort, proteins, localization_threshold)`. Panels are versioned identifier sets, not hard-coded gene pairs. Discovery operations return donor-level abundance/embedding tables, measured-feature masks, coverage and assay QC, module summaries, development contrasts and nearest development profiles. Each output carries source, preprocessing and model hashes and an exploratory designation. A phosphosite table retains accession, isoform, residue position, localization confidence and parent-protein coverage; ambiguous sites remain unresolved.

Three concrete uses follow the actual frozen corpus:

- Papers #56 (NUAK1–MYPT1/PP1β–GSK3β–PLK4) and #47 (ATM/ATR–KIFC1 phosphorylation) motivate querying centrosome/mitotic protein panels and available sites across independent PDAC tumors. Missing PLK4 or a specific KIFC1 site is reported as unmeasured, never inferred from a related transcript. The non-PDAC context of a mechanistic paper must remain attached to its hypothesis.
- Papers #82 (NRF2 and translation) and #94 (CPEB1, NRF2 proteostasis and ferroptosis) motivate protein stress/redox profiles, translation-module comparisons and retrieval of development tumors with similar measured programs. RNA–protein discordance can be a later discovery operation where separate measurements exist.
- Papers #64–65 motivate integrin/FAK-associated protein and site profiling. Total protein/phosphosite measurements do not establish the specified O-glycan state, phosphorylation occupancy, kinase activity or causal invasiveness.

Choose **protein-only independent-group comparison first**, with optional phosphosite discovery. It avoids losing most confirmation candidates to cross-omic intersections and avoids generating groups from the very proteome being tested. First proposed registered endpoint: equality of the frozen measured mitotic/stress protein representation between independent, untreated primary PDAC donors with externally assigned histological grades G1/G2 versus G3. Panel, grading crosswalk, treatment eligibility and missing-grade exclusion must be frozen from development before confirmation. This is a distributional association with grade, not an effect of grade or a directional kinase claim. Histology interpretation must be independent of the tested molecular scores. If compatible grade metadata or adequate groups cannot be verified, this endpoint is unavailable; do not substitute a favorable molecular subtype.

Fudan reports label-free quantification, unlike CPTAC's reference-based assay concern. Its availability statement assigns raw proteomics/phosphoproteomics to iProX IPX0002796002/IPX0002796001. The paper links supplementary protein tables and uses cohort-wide detection filtering; these matrices cannot automatically serve sequential confirmation. It reports site localization probability at least 0.75. Supplement availability is verified at the publication-link level; downloads and identity mapping are unaudited. Its CPTAC reanalysis supplies no new independent donors. These assay and access details come from the [primary methods and availability statement](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13045-022-01384-3).

[NCI's CPTAC access documentation](https://gdc.cancer.gov/about-gdc/contributed-genomic-data-cancer-research/clinical-proteomic-tumor-analysis-consortium-cptac) separates PDC proteomics from GDC genomics and distinguishes open clinical/expression data from controlled sequencing. Do not assume the entire multimodal resource is openly downloadable.

Before numerical access, an operator creates an inventory with accession/release, file hashes/license/access, donor→specimen→aliquot→run mapping, assay/reference/batch, treatment/grade definitions, and measurement-processing dependencies. Count sequentially: records; unique tumor donors; untreated PDAC; compatible metadata; eligible measured panels; group sizes. Treat counts from publication tables as ceilings. Keep future confirmation inventory and exclusions private. Public does not mean untouched: audit prior analysis, model pretraining and hypothesis selection, including published cohort-derived findings. If that history cannot support the stated confirmatory interpretation, retain Fudan as an external benchmark and seek a new cohort.

Reconstruct transformations from per-sample inputs: fixed protein identifiers, log transform/pseudocount, training-only center/scale, fixed imputation plus observed-value masks, and fixed coverage thresholds. Never fit ComBat, quantile maps, feature detection filters or imputation across confirmation donors. Within-donor normalization can preserve donor independence but changes the measured target and can introduce compositional effects. Audit protein grouping, match-between-runs, pooled identification, reference construction and batch fitting separately. For CPTAC shared references, conditioning is acceptable only with a documented conditional sampling argument; distinct donor IDs alone do not supply it. For Fudan, label-free acquisition does not prove independent processing. Missingness is part of the measured representation and must not be interpreted automatically as biological abundance change.

## Encoder, bettor and native process

Implement a modality-specific masked denoising encoder: fixed 2,000–8,000 protein inputs selected on TRAIN, width 256, latent dimension 32 or 64, observed-entry reconstruction loss and a mask channel. Select architecture and corruption rate on donor-disjoint validation and whole-cohort holdouts. Fit assay-specific transforms on development only; do not align Fudan using its aggregate distribution. Frozen rank-based inputs are a transfer baseline if ratio and label-free scales differ substantially. A separately trained phosphosite encoder comes after measured coverage justifies it; neither encoder pretends protein ratios are TPM.

Use the shared independent-group kernel exactly: a predictable witness gives `h_t = tanh(clip(g_t(phi(X_t))-g_t(phi(Y_t)), -4, 4))`, and log wealth adds `log1p(h_t)`. Under the registered conditional exchangeability null the odd contrast has conditional mean zero. This is native evidence, with no p-to-e step. Donors are units; peptides, sites, runs and technical replicates are not. One tumor per donor is selected by an outcome-independent rule. Paired tumor/normal samples never enter opposite independent groups.

First retain the existing two-burn-in-batch schedule: fit on earlier training batches, select early stopping on the most recent past batch, then score the next unopened batch. Store exact training/validation/scoring unit sets. At default eight pairs per batch, require the current 48-pair policy, and report the actual scored-pair count. The encoder remains frozen. A development-pretrained critic scoring the first confirmation pair is a later explicitly validated schedule, not a silent sample-size workaround. Do not lower the threshold merely because grade imbalance makes the cohort unusable. External critic transfer may fail even when the encoder reconstructs well.

The process uses the **one shared kernel and canonical donor/segment ledger** specified in the parent contract. Register null/population/panel, model and transformation hashes, order/stopping rule, exclusions, family and parent experiment. Receipt aliases, reordered arrays and new assay releases cannot reset consumption or wealth. First deliver deterministic finite-cohort replay; append mode waits for the shared atomic cursor, consumed-ID, optimizer/RNG and wealth transaction. Export final or prespecified stopped wealth privately, never substitute running maximum or multiply different panels/tools. Future cross-omic association must use separate frozen views and the canonical two-donor four-term kernel, not a jointly mixed encoder.

## Resource plan and simple comparisons

The parent verified an available L40S; no runtime has been measured. Proposed pilot cap: 30 GPU minutes and 16 GiB peak VRAM on a small training-only slice, after a CPU/PCA run. Record loading time, donors/second, epoch time, VRAM, CPU RAM, disk bytes, numerical failures and masked reconstruction on held-out donors. Project the complete run from those measurements. Stop if estimated training exceeds four GPU-hours or 32 GiB VRAM; revise width/features/batching before any larger budget. Raw mass-spectrometry reprocessing requires a separate storage/CPU estimate and authorization, not an assumed inexpensive preprocessing step.

Compare fixed module means, missingness-only classifiers, training-fitted PCA, linear witness and kernel witness at identical donor budgets and frozen panels. Conventional donor-level effects/permutation diagnostics remain useful development baselines but do not become native wealth by calibration. Compare reconstruction, retrieval stability, transfer, native power and detection delay. Train at most three development-selected seeds; lock one before confirmation, never select by hidden wealth. A learned model must demonstrate a useful improvement; a small cohort may favor PCA.

## Implementation milestones and validation

1. **Audit and schema.** Proposed `protein_design.py` and `scripts/audit_protein_cohort.py` emit an eligibility report without exposing confirmation data. Define `ProteinObservation-v1` with donor/specimen provenance, assay/scale, ordered feature IDs, values, mask, QC and source hashes. Define `ProteinEncoder-v1` with non-pickle weights, feature/preprocessing hashes, training donor keys, objective and software versions. Freeze the grading protocol after audit.
2. **Discovery pilot.** Add `protein_encoder.py`, `protein_tools.py`, `scripts/train_protein_encoder.py` and a `protein-tool profile|neighbors|compare|sites` CLI. Development artifacts contain provenance and exploratory status; no `ToolResult` confirmation claim. Update architecture/method documentation when behavior lands.
3. **Private native adapter.** Add `protein_experiment.py` registering `protein-group-native-v1` with `experiment_transport.QueueStore`; reuse registry hashing, immutable inputs, alias handling and private worker conventions from `registered_expression_scoring.py`. Submission contains registered references and returns only durable receipt. `learned_evalue.py` supplies the schedule/math seam; `expr_encoder.py` supplies provenance/serialization patterns, not its TPM transformation. The shared core owns new ledger/state code, avoiding a second queue.
4. **Validation and release.** Test mathematically generated fresh-donor nulls, shifted/sparse/nonlinear alternatives, missingness-only effects, batch shifts, duplicated donors, same-donor tissue swaps, current-batch training and global-normalization leakage. Across at least 10,000 cheap null streams report final and anytime rejection with binomial uncertainty at alpha 0.05, plus tail diagnostics; simulation supports but cannot replace the conditional-mean argument. Measure power on prespecified effects at the audited donor budget. Reject unsupported sampling even if simulations look calibrated.

Replay tests cover reordered aliases, interrupted writes, stale checkpoints and exactly-once factors. Privacy tests inspect HTTP, stdout/stderr, artifacts, journals, recall and timing/completion proxies; a same-user worker alone is insufficient containment. Existing falsifier and promotion behavior must remain unchanged.

Release discovery functionality when provenance, coverage and useful development operations pass. Release private evidence only after an independent sampling/preprocessing review, verified sufficient groups, measured resource gates, acceptable null diagnostics, and at least 80% simulated power for the declared development-derived minimum effect. Otherwise ship an explicitly exploratory tool and mark evidence unavailable. Outstanding decisions are compatible grade metadata, actual eligible counts, untouched-cohort status, assay normalization validity, platform transfer and deployment-enforced privacy. None is solved by additional GPU training.
