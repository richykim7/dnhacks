# Independent reviewer 1

Independent recommendation, before seeing other reviewers. This is a parent-edited summary of the returned proposal, not a consensus vote supplied to the reviewer.

Ranking: **pharmacotranscriptomic association → proteomic/phosphosignaling experiments → donor-level tumor–stroma state experiments**. Dependency-profile association ranked fourth because independent PDAC confirmation models may be scarce.

The leading tool would learn molecular/response representations and offer response curves, model similarity, program contributions and sensitivity-marker hypotheses on development data. Confirmation tests molecular-state/response independence on two fresh donor-derived models per update. A joint witness evaluates matched and crossed molecular/response pairings; an odd bounded function of their difference changes sign under swapping the two responses. Under the specified independence null and independent identically distributed fresh donors, this supplies one fair factor per two-donor block. The four witness evaluations are not four independent observations. Preprocessing and encoder tuning are frozen outside confirmation; the witness updates after scoring.

Proteomics adds protein abundance, phosphosite patterns and RNA–protein discordance beyond the expression tool. CPTAC reports 140 cancers (135 PDAC), 67 paired adjacent tissues and nine additional duct controls. An independent Korean study describes multiomic analyses involving 150 patients; this is not the eligible count for every modality. Cross-study development/confirmation is plausible after identity/platform/access audits. Adjacent-normal pairing is not independent-group sampling or randomized treatment.

Tumor–stroma tools would learn donor-level bags of cell states and offer compartment maps, compositions and associations. Hwang reports 43 primary PDAC specimens with processed single-nucleus/spatial resources; specimens must still be deduplicated to donors. Cell/ROI counts cannot satisfy the current 48-pairs default. This is a strong exploratory capability with a weaker near-term confirmation case.

Shared caveats: no verified untouched confirmation cohort yet; pooled assay/TMT/global normalization may violate sequential conditioning; model aliases, repeated releases and prior outcome exposure matter. GPU use belongs in representation learning, not a claim that more cells yield more independent evidence. Keep rich development outputs separate from private confirmation. Reuse queue, artifacts, donor provenance and learned-expression scheduling; add a genuine native independence kernel and modality contracts. Do not pool wealth across hypotheses or overlapping donors.
