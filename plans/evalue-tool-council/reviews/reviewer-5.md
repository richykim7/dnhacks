# Independent reviewer 5

Independent recommendation, after reading all corpus manifest titles but no other proposals. Parent-edited summary.

Ranking: **proteogenomic coupling → donor-level tumor–stroma ecosystems → learned pharmacotype association**. Image phenotyping is a data-contingent reserve.

The shared proposed native independence wager evaluates a learned compatibility function on matched and crossed pairs of two fresh donors. An odd bounded transform of the matched-minus-crossed contrast changes sign under response swapping. Under the registered independence null and independent identically distributed fresh units, its conditional mean is zero. Score one factor for the whole two-donor block, then learn from past blocks. A pretrained critic can score the first block only when trained and selected outside confirmation. Arbitrary continuous-covariate adjustment is not supplied by residualization.

Proteogenomic operations include protein/PTM profiles, coupling of independently encoded molecular layers, and independent-group state comparisons. The corpus specifically motivates nutrient/redox protein programs versus mitotic phosphorylation programs. Separate encoders must not mix the two views before an independence hypothesis is defined. CPTAC is a development candidate; Fudan's principal analysis reports 217 paired PDAC cases, within a larger recruitment inventory with assay-specific counts. A proposed split of roughly 100/40 CPTAC records is illustrative, not verified eligibility. Fudan's reanalysis of CPTAC is not another independent cohort. TMT/reference dependence, missingness, purity and platform overlap require audits.

Ecosystem tools would summarize compartments and couple malignant-cell stress states with fibroblast metabolic/ECM states across patients. Fixed cell representations and donor aggregation add cell-state distributions and cross-compartment biology beyond bulk expression. Composition denominators can create mathematical coupling; use separately defined compartment state summaries and report the exact measured null. Hwang reports 43 primary specimens. A 2025 atlas integrates 229 samples from 12 studies (172 primary, 25 metastatic, 26 adjacent-normal and six healthy); it is not 229 new independent patients. Exclude all held-out component studies/donors from training and reference fitting. Hwang alone supplies at most roughly 21 two-donor blocks before exclusions, so power must be measured with an explicit small-cohort design rather than inflating cell counts.

Pharmacotype operations would produce fixed dose/time response vectors and test nonlinear association with molecular state. PRISM is broad training/development material. Tiriac generated 114 cultures from 101 patients, but drug- and modality-specific subsets are smaller; even confirmed-PDAC culture counts are not final joined independent-donor denominators. Learn molecular/response encoders and a cross-modal critic on donor-disjoint development data, then score untouched PDOs. No synergy, mechanism or clinical-benefit claim follows from profile association.

Reuse private transport, strict manifests, artifact identities, replay and alias handling. Expose useful discovery profiles, maps and candidate hypotheses; keep confirmation measurements, effects, wealth, exclusions and completion signals private. Retain current falsifier and human promotion boundaries. Never replace final/stopped wealth with running maximum or pool unrelated/null-overlapping analyses. Current architecture's execution/storage isolation still needs actual deployment enforcement.
