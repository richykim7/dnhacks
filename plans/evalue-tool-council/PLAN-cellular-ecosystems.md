## Reusable biological operations

Build a count-aware cell/set representation tool with these parameterized operations:

- `profile`: select organism, tissue, assay, donor population, compartment ontology and frozen state dictionary; return donor-level state distributions, heterogeneity, representative gene programs, cell coverage and missingness. Preserve continuous embeddings alongside interpretable state labels.
- `couple`: select two separately defined compartments and state panels; return exploratory donor association maps and effect summaries on development data. Examples include malignant stress distributions versus fibroblast metabolic states, or malignant antigen-presentation states versus immune states.
- `compare`: compare a selected compartment's donor distributions between independently sampled populations. Return development effects and coverage; the shared two-sample kernel is a subsequent evidence adapter. Paired tissues require a different reviewed contract.
- `spatial-profile`: where coordinates, segmentation and assay resolution exist, select distance scale, compartment pair and neighborhood rule; return donor summaries of state neighborhoods and uncertainty. Dissociated cells cannot supply spatial locations; GeoMx regions are not single-cell neighborhoods.

The actual frozen corpus card concerns division, nutrient use, redox/protein stress and stroma. Its manifest identifies #9 (stellate-cell alanine secretion) and #21 (alanine transporters), motivating transporter/stress distributions within malignant cells and metabolic fibroblast distributions. Papers #97–98 motivate inflammatory, myofibroblast and antigen-presenting states; #99–100 motivate lactate/redox-associated fibroblast heterogeneity. Paper #87 motivates malignant antigen-presentation versus immune-state hypotheses. These are reusable parameter selections, not fixed weighted pathway tests. Expression associations cannot establish alanine flux, ligand transfer, signaling direction or treatment causality; those require additional measurements/interventions.

Development outputs include donor maps, embedding coverage, state examples, subsampling stability, exploratory associations and literature-linked candidate questions. They remain useful even when confirmation is unavailable. PRIVATE confirmation instead accepts immutable hypothesis/model/data references and returns only a durable receipt. All outcomes, exclusions, effects, errors and wealth paths stay with the private operator; discovery never receives hidden cohort rankings or success proxies.

## First null and measured observations

Start with cross-compartment association in one predeclared untreated, primary-PDAC assay population. For donor d, X_d is a frozen summary of malignant-cell state distribution and Y_d a separately frozen fibroblast-state summary. The first null is **independence of these measured representations among eligible donors in that population**. It is not independence of latent biological states after removing all confounding. Treatment mixtures, sampling sites and assay differences can produce associations; restrict the first population rather than claim arbitrary conditional independence from residualization.

Each independent donor contributes one pair of bags. Multiple resections, sections, lanes, nuclei and organoids remain nested measurements. Freeze one specimen-selection or aggregation rule per donor, including time point. Related subjects or pooled preparations need an explicit sampling justification. Distinct donor strings alone do not establish independence.

Use the common two-view kernel unchanged. On two fresh IID donor pairs, score a past-measurable critic c_t in [-1,1]:

```
h_t = (c_t(X1,Y1) + c_t(X2,Y2) - c_t(X1,Y2) - c_t(X2,Y1))/4
W_t = W_(t-1) * (1 + lambda_t*h_t), 0 <= lambda_t <= 0.9
```

Under the registered null the four conditional expectations coincide, so the positive factor has conditional mean one. One two-donor block produces one factor. Critic, stake, gene selection, encoders and preprocessing must be fixed before that block is revealed. Score first, then allow critic updates using consumed past blocks only; preserve a fully frozen critic baseline. This applies the shared construction grounded in [sequential independence betting](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/podkopaev23a.html), not p-to-e conversion. A randomized ordering of a cohort already inspected to choose its hypothesis does not restore the IID/private contract.

## Representation and measurement safeguards

Train separate malignant and fibroblast count encoders: negative-binomial reconstruction with measured library-size offsets, training-only gene selection and dispersion fitting, modest 32–64-dimensional embeddings. Use masked-gene reconstruction and donor-balanced batches. Begin with frozen cell embeddings aggregated by mean, variance and state occupancy; test a small permutation-invariant learned set aggregator trained on same-compartment reconstruction/subbag stability. Compare against pseudobulk PCA, fixed state summaries and fixed kernel witnesses. Training is worthwhile only if it improves transferable distribution sensitivity or biological utility.

No joint malignant/fibroblast encoder, cross-view attention or whole-donor concatenation may feed these two association views. External critic training may learn their association; the mappings themselves remain separate. Freeze weights, library transforms, labels, reference cells and alignment. An implementation inspired by [scVI's count model](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-018-0229-2) needs its own artifact and validation; the current `FrozenEncoder` accepts TPM and cannot silently ingest UMI counts.

Specific safeguards:

- Composition is a separate endpoint. Two fractions sharing total captured cells can be coupled mechanically. The first null uses within-compartment states and separate denominators, with fixed per-compartment subsampling; exclude whole-tissue fractions and shared totals from critic inputs. This reduces a failure mode without proving absence of sampling-induced dependence.
- Finite-cell sampling defines the measured phenotype. Freeze minimum cells, coverage and subsampling seeds using development studies. Report subbag variance privately or on exposed development data; repeated subsamples improve measurement, never donor n. Simulate shared capture-depth noise even when inputs omit depth.
- Missing compartments are unavailable measurements, not zero biological abundance. A complete-case cohort is a selected population whose selection can induce dependence. Register eligibility explicitly and test selection mechanisms; if biological independence is the intended null, unexplained informative selection blocks confirmation.
- No joint integration, Harmony fitting, normalization reference, batch-statistic update or transductive label transfer using confirmation donors. Single-donor inference uses frozen training references. Strong batch confounding or unhandled scRNA/snRNA domain shift fails closed; development batch holdouts must expose it.
- Spatial extensions need separate assay-specific representations and a donor aggregation rule. Mixed spots can manufacture apparent coexpression. No spatial confirmation in release one; demonstrate donor-level spatial summaries before proposing a new spatial null.

## Scarce donors, power and compute

Forty-three specimens provide at most 21 blocks; the reported untreated subset gives at most nine before donor/coverage exclusions. The current expression default's 48 pairs is an implementation policy, not a theorem; retain its behavior and propose a separately versioned small-cohort contract. An externally developed critic can score block one without spending scarce confirmation donors on burn-in. With lambda <=0.9, even maximal factors need five blocks to cross 20; nine ideal blocks could reach at most 1.9^9 = 322.7. This necessary bound is far from a realistic power claim. The narrow untreated population avoids treatment-mixture dependence being mistaken for intrinsic ecosystem coupling. A separate all-primary, treatment-stratified design may be assessed before outcomes, with its own sampling proof and budget; do not silently widen the population to cross a threshold.

Before private release simulate 18, 24, 40, 60 and 100 independent donors, varying nonlinear state coupling, rare-state frequency, cell counts, dropout, batch confounding and missing-compartment selection. Compare frozen simple/kernel/PCA/learned critics at equal donor budgets. Report final/anytime rejection, binomial uncertainty, power and detection-delay distributions including noncrossers. Use at least 10,000 independent null streams at alpha .05, plus invalid current-block training, shared-denominator and cell-pseudoreplication controls. Predeclare a useful alternative on development data and require 80% power at the available donor budget before advertising a confirmatory capability. Otherwise release development profiling with confirmation explicitly unavailable for that design.

The centrally checked L40S had 46,068 MiB and was idle at observation; it is not reserved. Proposed pilot: one GPU, <=100,000 training cells, 2,000–4,000 genes, batches 256–512, 20 epochs, <=2 GPU-hours initial cap. Measure throughput, peak memory, host RAM and validation loss after one epoch; stop on nonfinite losses or projected overrun. Permit a <=12 GPU-hour comparison only after the pilot, training-only access audit and donor-held-out utility gain justify it. Stream sparse counts; never densify the atlas in memory. Reserve <=32 GiB VRAM initially and compare CPU inference. Implementation update: separate real Peng/Lin development PCA/NB/set pilots have now run on CPU; see the training report. No GPU expansion was justified by these results.
